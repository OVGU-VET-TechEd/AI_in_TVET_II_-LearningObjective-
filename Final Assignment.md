## Slide 8 – What AI Cannot Do

- Pedagogical judgement

- Final grading decisions

## 🛠️ Hands-On Activity (10 minutes)

**Part A: Draft Your AI Prompt (3 min)**

Use this template:

**Subject/Skill:** [What do you teach?]
Example: Electrical installation, basic wiring

**EQF Level:** [Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced]

**Lesson Duration:** 45 minutes

**Your Prompt:**
"You are an experienced TVET teacher.
Create a [SUBJECT] lesson for [LEVEL] students.
Include:
1. Clear learning objective
2. One practical activity
3. One assessment task aligned to the objective

Time: [DURATION] minutes"

### Part B: Run Your Prompt (3 min)

1. Go to: chatgpt.com (or paste in provided text)
2. Copy-paste your prompt
3. Read the output

### Slide 14  Review & Improve (4 min)

Check these 5 things:

- [ ] Learning objective is **SMART** (Specific, Measurable)?
- [ ] Activity is **realistic** for TVET (real tools/equipment)?
- [ ] Assessment **matches** the objective?
- [ ] Instructions are **clear** for EQF level?
- [ ] **What would YOU change?** (write 1 thing)


## Slide 15 – AI and Assessment in TVET

AI can support assessment by:
- Creating quiz questions
- Drafting rubrics
- Suggesting practical tasks

But **teachers must validate everything**.

---

## Slide 16 – Risks of AI in Assessment

Possible risks:
- Bias in questions
- Over-simplified tasks
- Misalignment with outcomes
- Over-reliance on AI

---

## Slide 17 – Ethical Use of AI

Responsible AI use means:
- Transparency
- Fairness
- Data protection
- Teacher control

Ethics are non-negotiable.

## Slide 18 – Your Turn: Design an Assessment Task

**Using the lesson objective YOU created (Slide 13), now design ONE assessment task.**

**AI Prompt Template:**

"You are a TVET assessment expert.
Create ONE practical assessment task for this learning objective:

[Paste your lesson objective from Slide 13]

The assessment task should:
1. Be PRACTICAL (test real hands-on skills, not theory)
2. Be FAIR (no hidden biases; clear to all students)
3. Be DOABLE (realistic in 20 minutes with available equipment)
4. Have CLEAR SUCCESS CRITERIA

Format: Task description + Success criteria (3-4 bullets)"

---

## Slide 19 – Evaluate Your Assessment Task

Check these questions:

- [ ] Does it measure your learning objective?
- [ ] Is it realistic for your TVET context?
- [ ] Could any student group struggle unfairly?
- [ ] Are success criteria crystal clear?
- [ ] What would YOU change before using it?
